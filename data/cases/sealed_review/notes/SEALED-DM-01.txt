Case ID: SEALED-DM-01

The driver has documented type 2 diabetes, explicitly recorded as non-gestational. A current medication list signed by the treating clinic shows an evening insulin dose; an older employer form still says diet control only. He brought six weeks of meter downloads and says the remaining monitoring record is on a damaged phone. He reports recognising tremor before meals, but the nurse has no specialist statement about hypoglycaemia awareness, severe events, end-organ effects or whether the regimen minimises risk. The nurse treats the current clinic list as the treatment source and requests the missing monitoring and specialist assessment.
