Case ID: SEALED-VIS-01

The driver brings a current optometry report that records right-eye and left-eye distance acuity in separate corrected and uncorrected columns. The report lists corrected right 6/9, corrected left 6/12, uncorrected right 6/18 and uncorrected left 6/24. The driver says the spectacles in the report are the pair used for work. On several evening trips he nevertheless noticed a second image beside reflective road signs; the report does not measure the angle of doubling or state whether covering one eye resolves it. The nurse enters the four acuity readings exactly as documented and keeps the intermittent visual symptom open for examination rather than treating the acuity chart as an explanation for it.
