Case ID: SEALED-HTN-02

A screening kiosk displayed 181/99 mmHg just after the driver lifted freight, but a seated clinic measurement twenty minutes later was 146/88 mmHg. The driver's phone contains an old photograph of a prescription label without a date; he cannot establish whether he is taking the medicine now. He reports that his brother has hypertension, while his own previous assessment was described as normal. The nurse does not convert one high kiosk reading or a relative's history into persistent hypertension and requests a dated series, treatment details and any relevant specialist information before the commercial review is completed.
