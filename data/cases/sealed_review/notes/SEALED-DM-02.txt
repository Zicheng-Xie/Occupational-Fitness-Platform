Case ID: SEALED-DM-02

The driver takes tablets for established diabetes and denies a current insulin prescription. After a missed meal, a colleague found him confused and gave him glucose because he could not find his own supply. The first meter reading was taken after treatment, and the driver cannot say whether he would have recognised the episode in time to respond alone. An emergency note mentions possible severe hypoglycaemia but does not settle its classification or date. The nurse documents the third-party assistance, uncertainty of the episode and current tablet treatment separately, and requests the treating specialist's assessment before a driving conclusion is made.
