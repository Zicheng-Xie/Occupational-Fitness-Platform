Case ID: SEALED-HEAR-01

A signed audiology report identifies the right ear as the better ear and gives unaided thresholds of 35, 40, 45 and 40 dB at 500, 1000, 2000 and 3000 Hz respectively. The report also records an aided assessment, but the driver brought only the first page and that later result is not visible. He says the depot radio is easier to understand with his hearing aids, although a supervisor's old form says hearing was satisfactory. The nurse records the measured unaided frequencies, notes the missing aided result and asks the audiologist to confirm any recommendation relevant to commercial driving.
