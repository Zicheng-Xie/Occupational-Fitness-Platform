Case ID: HOLD-VIS-01

During night deliveries the driver sometimes sees a second image beside an oncoming vehicle. Closing either eye removes the doubling, but he was symptom-free during this morning's appointment. A routine chart reading was recorded without noting whether glasses were worn, and no examiner measured the position of the double image relative to straight-ahead vision. The nurse does not treat a normal office conversation as proof that the driving symptom has resolved.
