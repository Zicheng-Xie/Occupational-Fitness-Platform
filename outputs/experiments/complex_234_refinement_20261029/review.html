What changed

Candidate retrieval and fusion

Method 3 uses wider measured vector/BM25 candidates. Method 4 retains its source/rule/section graph candidate ranking. Method 7 expands its original fact-planned search frontier. All can use up to six complete, verbatim disease-scoped sentences, with a fixed RRF weight shared across the added views.

Method 9 replays its actual Neo4j response frontier; method 10 replays its actual PageIndex navigated original leaves. Their new facet scores and allocation are first-party post-retrieval refinements, with no new graph/tree navigation or hidden native substitute.

Original evidence allocation

Only same-page parents containing complete retrieved originals can consolidate overlapping evidence. New ranking preserves previous whole-original coverage at four prefix depths. The selection objective uses typed criterion coverage and total original coverage, then measured retrieval relevance; no expected answers enter ranking.

Original methods, all previous results, unchanged notes and production settings remain preserved. New local sentence embeddings were actually computed by Ollama; raw requests and responses are included.

Gains and limits

Method 10 reaches 90.29% coverage R@5 and 87.02% case packet coverage. Method 4 reaches 95.18% coverage R@10. Method 3 mainly improves R@10. Methods 7 and 9 retain their earlier R@5; their gains are in R@10.

The metrics do not all improve. Exact-ID recall declines because a complete parent original replaces directly returned child IDs; method 4 also uses more original text. Do not interpret broader original-text coverage as universal retrieval superiority or improved fitness judgment accuracy.

For methods 7 and 9, an additional joint-selection experiment examined 26,410,318 five-passage combinations in 714 conditions. It produced no additional change. This result concerns the current candidate pool, nested preservation constraints and typed/all-original coverage objective; it does not establish a limit on patient-aware relevance ranking or other retrieval architectures.

The paired ablation is retained: allocation alone versus allocation plus sentence views. Sentence views do not increase R@5 for methods 7 or 9 on this cohort. This is a development-set observation, with correlated synthetic families and pending independent clinical annotation.

Validation and evidence trail

17,136 nested whole-original coverage checks passed across the final 4,284 conditions. The first implementation attempt contained a source-swap defect: 1,164 of its 3,570 conditions were rejected. That full attempt and its exact source snapshots remain in initial_attempt. The defect was fixed, all primary conditions rerun, and no favorable failed-run results were substituted.

Recorded optimization timing excludes reused native graph/tree navigation, intake and cached embeddings. This experiment does not claim an end-to-end latency improvement. Clinical decision models were not called.
