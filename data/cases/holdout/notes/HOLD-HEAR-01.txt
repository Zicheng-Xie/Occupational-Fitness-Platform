Case ID: HOLD-HEAR-01

The driver follows face-to-face speech while wearing hearing aids but misses radio calls in a running vehicle. A phone image of an audiogram shows some thresholds, although the ear labels and the 3000 Hz row are cut off. It is unclear whether the aids were in place during testing. The nurse cannot calculate an unaided better-ear average from the photograph and requests the signed audiology record.
