Case ID: SEALED-HEAR-02

The driver repeatedly asks for instructions to be repeated when a ventilation fan is running, but follows quiet conversation without difficulty. He shows a phone screen reading 43 dB with no named ear, date, test frequencies or indication of whether his hearing aid was worn. His spouse reports recent missed doorbell sounds, while an employment form from the previous year marked hearing normal. The nurse records the functional concern and conflicting informal observations without assigning an unaided better-ear average. A formal audiogram and specialist interpretation are requested.
