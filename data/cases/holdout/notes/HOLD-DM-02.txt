Case ID: HOLD-DM-02

A driver taking glucose-lowering medication became confused after a missed meal and a coworker gave him a sugary drink because he could not respond to the meter alarm. No glucose reading was captured until after treatment, and there is no clinician's account of the episode. He denies losing consciousness. The nurse records the assistance and uncertainty separately, then asks for assessment of severe hypoglycaemia and the related commercial-driving restrictions.
