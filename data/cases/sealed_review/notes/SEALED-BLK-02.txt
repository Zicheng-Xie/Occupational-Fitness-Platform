Case ID: SEALED-BLK-02

The driver describes an episode during a blood donation in which he briefly lost consciousness after the needle was inserted. The treating clinic's signed note calls it provoked vasovagal syncope and records recovery after lying down. A separate unsworn account from a coworker mentions the driver appearing distracted during a shift months later, but neither loss of consciousness nor the date is established for that event. The nurse records the diagnosed provoked episode and the unrelated allegation as separate evidence, and asks the reviewing clinician whether further investigation of the second account is needed.
