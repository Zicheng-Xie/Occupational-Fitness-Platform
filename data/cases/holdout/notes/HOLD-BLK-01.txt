Case ID: HOLD-BLK-01

After an unresponsive spell at a loading bay, the driver remembers sitting down but not a coworker calling his name. The witness is unsure whether there were two episodes or one prolonged episode. An ECG has been arranged and a neurology referral remains open; neither clinician has established a mechanism. The nurse records the possibility of transient loss of awareness without assigning it to a cardiac, neurological or metabolic diagnosis.
