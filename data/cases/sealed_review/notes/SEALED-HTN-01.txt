Case ID: SEALED-HTN-01

The driver presents for a commercial licence medical after a roster change. A dated clinic sheet contains three seated readings taken on different days over the past fortnight: 174/102, 172/104 and 176/101 mmHg. He says he took his prescribed antihypertensive on each of those days, but has not brought the dispensing record. Today's repeat readings are recorded on a separate sheet that the nurse has not yet received. He describes a brief headache after a long shift, without a documented neurological finding. The nurse retains the dated readings as a series, records the treatment claim separately and asks the treating clinician to check the missing sheet and any driving-relevant complications.
