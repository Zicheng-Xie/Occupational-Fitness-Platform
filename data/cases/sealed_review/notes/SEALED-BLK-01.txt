Case ID: SEALED-BLK-01

While waiting in a hot warehouse, the driver became pale and sat on the floor. One witness says he answered throughout; another recalls about ten seconds when he did not respond. The driver remembers feeling nauseated but cannot describe the disputed interval. A similar event is mentioned in an earlier referral without a date. A cardiac monitor has been fitted and the report is pending; no clinician has confirmed vasovagal syncope, seizure or another mechanism. The nurse documents the witness disagreement, investigation status and uncertain event count without combining them into a settled diagnosis.
